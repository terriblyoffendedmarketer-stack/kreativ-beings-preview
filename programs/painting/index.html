Painting & art classes

Rang means colour. You bring the nerve, we bring the brushes, the paint and a very patient teacher.

Our painting afternoons are led by Mohini Sinha, an acrylic artist and art educator who also teaches online on Skillshare. Watercolour, acrylic florals and landscapes, miniature acrylics: every workshop is built for complete beginners and is just as much fun for people who already paint.

Our October workshop had teenagers painting next to a wonderful 70-years-young participant, with sandwiches and cold coffee on the side. Create. Sip. Connect.

There is also Come Paint With Us, a monthly meet-up for artists to paint together, and a weekend Kids Art Class for ages 5 to 12.

A slow afternoon. A little colour. A lot of joy.From our October workshop

Good to know

I've never painted. Is that okay?

It's the ideal starting point. Every workshop is designed for beginners.

What do I bring?

Nothing but yourself. All painting materials are provided.

Do you have classes for children?

Yes. The Kids Art Class runs on weekends for ages 5 to 12, and workshops are open from age 10.
