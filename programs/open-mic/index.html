Open mic nights

Ghar means home, and that's what a good open mic should feel like: a place where your words are welcome exactly as they are.

Kreativ Beings hosts open mics with our friends at Ghar by Tape a Tale and Awaken Words. There have been evenings of poetry, storytelling, shayari and music, a Diwali special, and Jugni, an open mic just for women.

Whatever you share, there is a space for your art and a room to share it with. Nervous first-timer or seasoned performer, the mic is the same height for everyone.

And the best part often comes afterwards, when people stay back, strike up conversations and find kindred spirits.

Some evenings are more than just an open mic. They become a collection of voices.Awaken Words, after Jugni
