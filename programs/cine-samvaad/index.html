Cine Samvaad

Samvaad means conversation. So yes, there is a film. But the real show starts when the lights come up and somebody says, "Okay, but did you believe that ending?"

Cine Samvaad is an intimate screening for a small room of film lovers: a thoughtfully curated film, sometimes paired with a short, followed by a proper conversation about the story, the characters, the craft and what it stirred up in each of us.

So far we have watched Iqbal (most of the room was seeing it for the first time), Chak De! India with the short film Moti, and Piku with the short We Cry Together. One regular called it "the kind of cinema that makes you forget you are watching a screen."

You don't need film-school vocabulary. "I liked it" is a fine place to begin. "I didn't" is even better, because it gets the room going.

Watch. Discuss. Reflect. Connect.The Cine Samvaad promise

Good to know

Do I need to know anything about cinema?

No. If you have an opinion about a film, even a half-formed one, you are qualified.

Which films do you screen?

Hand-picked Hindi films with heart, so far Iqbal, Chak De! India and Piku, often with a short film alongside.

How do I find the next date?

Dates go up on our Instagram and on the ticket listing.
